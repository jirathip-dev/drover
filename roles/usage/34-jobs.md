## Jobs

#1756: a scheduled job is one config entry: `every` (`"12h"`, `"1d"`, `"7d"`; `0` or `"off"`: only by hand), an optional `check` command, the issue's `title` and `body`, its `role`, its `queue` place (`top`, default, or `end`) and an optional `empty` line. Built in: `lesson` (7d), `triage` (1d), `deps` (30d), `advisory` (1d) and `integration` (off; #1785: the premerge gate once per integration head, a red head files `integration: <fleet> red at <sha>` with the PRs since the last green, a green head closes it), each in `roles/jobs/`; `[job.<name>]` and `[fleet.<n>.job.<name>]` override them key by key or add a job. `tick` runs each due job of each fleet not paused: its check runs in a checkout of the integration head (it passes: nothing to do; it fails: its output is the issue's `{report}`), then at most one open issue per job and never a title that exists, then the issue is filed (`job-filed`), queued and its role spawned; a refused spawn leaves it queued and drive starts it with that role. A job that fails to file logs `job-failed`. `drover job <fleet>` lists the jobs (any lane); `drover job <fleet> <name>` runs one now (orchestrators and the owner only): its check's verdict is ignored, at most one still holds. A job with `empty` set lets its lane end with `drover done --no-change "<line>" <fleet> <issue>`: drover closes the issue with that line and the undo of each change the lane logged.
