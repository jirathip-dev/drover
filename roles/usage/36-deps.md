## Deps

#1488: the `deps` job files `deps: <fleet> <year>-<month>` and spawns a `deps` lane (impl's harness unless `[fleet.<n>.role.deps]` sets one). The lane bumps every direct dependency of every ecosystem (Cargo, pnpm or npm, SwiftPM, sha-pinned GitHub Actions, pinned tools) to its latest release in one PR, fixes what breaks, leaves out (and lists with the reason) a bump it cannot make green, and adds or removes no dependency. Its PR body carries a table (dependency, old, new, each lockfile's package count before and after) and goes through the normal gates and review. The `advisory` job's check is the repo's audit (`cargo deny check advisories`, `pnpm audit`, `npm audit`) on the integration head: when it fails, one `deps: advisories <date>` issue carries the tool's output and its `deps` lane fixes every advisory in one PR.
