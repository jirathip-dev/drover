Closes #1169

## For humans
Text you type in a thread now stays put, whether the box is focused or not, while messages arrive, on the 30-second refresh, when you come back to the app and after a reload.
New messages are added in place instead of redrawing the whole thread, so there is no flash, no image reload and no scroll jump.
When the phone's storage for the page is full, old cached screens are dropped so your draft is still saved.

## Parity
none: no row of docs/parity.md is changed or dropped. Row 2 (scroll never jumps) stays `open #1084`; its ignored `parity_2` is the Events tab's page scroll, not the thread view.

## Cause
A composer that was not focused was rebuilt on every repaint and got its text back only from the draft saved in local storage. With storage full of cached screens (a used phone), that save fails silently, so the rebuilt box came back empty. The repro fills storage and blurs the box: on the base the text is `""` after the stream and 7 nodes were replaced.

## What changed
- `web/src/blocks.ts` `morph()`: a repaint patches the page in place, children keyed by `data-id`; a block whose JSON and open state are unchanged keeps its node (field, focus, scroll, image); a changed leaf block is replaced, containers (screen, section, list, sheet, #1120's `.msgs`) are patched.
- `web/src/main.ts`: the focus-only graft (`input, select` match) is deleted; the first fetch after painting the cached copy highlights no row.
- `web/src/theme.ts` `put()`: a full storage drops the `scr:` caches and retries, so a draft is always saved.
- CI: `ui/repaint.mjs` changes trigger ui-webkit.
