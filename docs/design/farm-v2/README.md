# Farm v2: horses act by role, a paddock per project, the hay-bale rail, moments, the board scheme (#242, phase 1)

`index.html` is the design sheet. It shows the Herd page, the paddocks, the actions × 3 coats with their row sprites and
ASCII art, the rail and moments, the weather, the board tabs and the contract draft. `board.html` is the board
(Queue, Host and Events, `?tab=`) in the farm colour scheme with the row sprites. `sprite-sheet.html` shows every sprite in
`sprites.svg`. Both pages link `assets/serve.css` (and `index.html` also links `herd.css`) unchanged. To view them, serve
the repo root (`python3 -m http.server`) and open `docs/design/farm-v2/`. `?still=1` gives the reduced-motion frames, and
`board.html?scheme=today` shows today's colours for comparison.

`sprites.svg` is generated by `make_sprites.py` (`python3 docs/design/farm-v2/make_sprites.py`, stdlib only). The horse is
farm's current one (Corral's `horsesvg.py` port in `assets/themes/farm/sprites.svg`, MIT © jirathip-dev). It keeps the
same body, neck, head and leg shapes, and the 8 coats as `--c/--d/--m/--b`. The legs are still `.leg` groups with `--a`
(herd.js's trot) and the manes still `var(--m)` (they skew in a storm). The sprites add `--fk`, the project colour, a bent
leg and the props.

Screenshots (headless Chromium, full page, `?still=1`): `farm-v2-390.png` (DPR 2), `farm-v2-1440.png`,
`sprite-sheet.png` (DPR 2), `board-queue-390.png`, `board-host-390.png`, `board-events-390.png` (DPR 2),

## Actions by role

Each sprite is 132 × 100, like today's poses. Each is shown in 3 coats (chestnut, black, grey), each coat in a different
fleet colour. The project colour is worn as a halter on the head and a saddle cloth on the back (on the plough, the collar
too; on the hay run, the pack pad and girth).

| contract key | role | sprite | what it shows | row sprite | ASCII word |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `impl` | impl | `horse-plough` | pulling a plough, the traces to the collar, a furrow behind; trots as today | `row-plough` | ploughing |
| `fix` | fix round | `horse-anvil` | at the anvil, the near hind hoof lifted, a hot shoe, sparks, the hammer | `row-anvil` | shoeing |
| `rev` | review | `horse-vet` | at the fence, the vet (white coat, stethoscope) holding a front hoof | `row-vet` | vet check |
| `update` | catch-up | `horse-hay` | 3 hay bales on a pack saddle, a walk | `row-hay` | hauling hay |
| `idle` | idle | `horse-graze` | grazing, the head down in the grass | `row-graze` | grazing |
| `done` | done | `horse-stable` | trotting into the stable: the front half is through the door, dimmed inside | `row-stable` | stabled |
| `blocked` | needs you, blocked, dead | `horse-rear` | rearing at the gate, red neigh marks at the mouth | `row-rear` | rearing |

**What changes from today:** idle grazes instead of standing, done walks into the stable instead of lowering its head, and
blocked rears at a gate. These follow your Q1, so they are deliberate changes under phase 2's "unless the mockup changes
them". Done keeps today's 75 % dim. Blocked drops today's red sepia tint so the coat and fleet colour stay visible: the
rear, the red neigh and the red `needs you` label carry the signal (see Q4). Dead keeps the tint.

**ASCII mode** (`modes: scene, ascii`): each action has 3 lines, at most 8 columns of plain ASCII, so it fits today's
48 × 40 avatar at 10 px. Each also has a word. The row `title` and accessible label stay the plain state word (`impl`,
`review(1)`). The word is flavour only, and the Herd labels leave it out (it truncated at 164 px).

## The paddock, the rail, the moments

- **Paddock per project:** each fleet's Herd page is its paddock. A post-and-rail fence runs along the pasture top
  (`paddock-bay`, a 52 × 30 tile repeated across the width), with its top rail in `--fk`. A name board sits on the gate
  post with an `--fk` edge. The colour is #162's: `color`, else `sha256(name)[0] % 5` over l/p/c/g/a (red left out). The
  demo gives acme-api green, acme-web cyan and docs blue.
- **Hay-bale rail:** the queued horses at the gate become one bale per queued issue (`bale`, 40 × 28 in today's 60 px
  gate slot), in queue order. The labels stay as they are (`#25`, `⛔#25`). A needs-you issue with no lane is
  `bale-red`, with a cream `!` tag and `! needs you` on its label.
- **Moments,** played once from the `/live` event, silently:
  - `merged`: the fence jump in 3 frames (`jump-takeoff`, `jump-over`, `jump-land`), about 0.5 s each;
  - `fail`: refusing the jump in 2 frames (`refuse-plant`, a dust puff, then `refuse-turn`);
  - `needs-you`: the bale turns red, with a 3 px bob.

  Under reduced motion each shows its outlined static frame only (`jump-over`, `refuse-plant`, the red bale, no bob).

## Weather, unchanged

The ranch strip is herd.js's own `scene()`/`clouds()`/`night()` code, copied unchanged into `index.html`. It is shown at
calm (load under 50 % of the limit), windy (≥ 50 %: trees, grass and clouds ×1–3, live in the HTML, frozen at a gust peak
in the PNGs) and storm (the spawn gate refuses: darker sky, rain, manes blown). Fog and night are untouched too.
`load_scene` maps #241's levels to these: `calm → calm`, `busy → windy`, `held → storm`. The Host tab's MACHINE header
gets the miniature (`mini-calm`, `mini-windy`, `mini-storm`, 40 × 16). The demo's 1.20 / 2.5 is calm.

## Row sprites (Queue and Events)

A 16 px pixel sprite replaces the state mark (`.gt b` on Queue, `.kg` on Events). The mark's word becomes the sprite's
`title` and label. Queue picks the sprite by the row's state word: `impl`, `review(n)`, `fix(n)`, `update(n)`, `queued`
(bale), needs-you or dead (rear), `parked` (graze). A needs-human item with no lane gets the red bale. Events picks it by
event type: `merged` → jump, verdict FAIL → refuse, verdict PASS → vet, `spawn` → its role's action, needs-human or
needs-decision → rear, `queue` → bale. Every other event keeps its glyph (`✗`, `·`, `▸`).

- The graph column grows from 1ch to 16 px so the sprite clears `#21`: `--gw: calc(2 * var(--d, 0) * 1ch + 16px)`. That
  rule is #241's row-sprite markup.
- Row sprites take their coat from `--rc/--rd/--rm` (palomino unless set inline), not `--c`. The board's `:root` defines
  `--c` as the cyan token, so an inherited `--c` paints every row horse cyan. Today's horses always set the coat inline,
  so nothing breaks now. #241 can set `--rc…` inline when the row has a lane.

## The farm board scheme (the 2026-11-03 comment, #244 Q2)

The scheme is custom properties on `<html>` while the theme is active (proposed: `theme.json` `scheme`). The background is
deep night-green, surfaces are soil, controls and the active tab are wheat-gold, and links are sky-blue. The status
colours stay green, amber and red. Amber moves to orange (`#f29b4b`) so it never reads as the wheat accent. The five
fleet colours keep their hues.

| token | today | farm | use | contrast: bg / soil / red card |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `--bg` | `#0d1117` | `#0f1a13` | background | – |
| `--sf` (new) | `#161b22` | `#1d1a12` | surfaces: blocks, `pre`, cards' boxes | – |
| `--edge` (new) | `#30363d` | `#3b3a27` | card and box borders | – |
| `--line` | `#21262d` | `#26301f` | row rules | – |
| `--fg` | `#c9d1d9` | `#ece4cc` | text, warm cream | 14.0 / 13.7 / 11.2 |
| `--mute` | `#8b949e` | `#a3a68a` | secondary text | 7.1 / 6.9 / 5.7 |
| `--ac` (new) | (`--l`) | `#e2bf5e` | `[controls]`, the active tab | 10.1 / 9.8 / 8.0 |
| `--l` | `#58a6ff` | `#7cc4f0` | links; fleet colour l | 9.4 / 9.1 / 7.4 |
| `--g` | `#3fb950` | `#7fd06a` | ok | 9.5 / 9.2 / 7.5 |
| `--a` | `#d29922` | `#f29b4b` | working, warn | 8.1 / 7.9 / 6.5 |
| `--r` | `#f85149` | `#ff7d6e` | needs you, failed | 7.1 / 7.0 / 5.7 |
| `--p` | `#bc8cff` | `#c7a4ff` | fleet colour p | 8.6 / 8.4 / 6.9 |
| `--c` | `#39c5cf` | `#5fd0c4` | fleet colour c | 9.6 / 9.4 / 7.6 |

All text is at least 5.7:1 (WCAG AA is 4.5), and the bg in the active tab's wheat fill is 10.1:1. The "red card" is the
needs-you card's 15 % red over the bg. The Herd overlay keeps its own scene colours. To apply the scheme, serve.css needs
3 token swaps: `#161b22 → var(--sf,#161b22)`, `#30363d → var(--edge,#30363d)`, and `var(--l)` → `var(--ac,var(--l))` on
buttons and the tab bar. `board.html` writes these out as overrides (see Q2).

## Open questions for the owner

1. **"Pixel-horse style":** today's farm horses are vector (Corral's port), not pixel art. I kept that vector style for
   everything 132 × 100, and only the 16 px row sprites are pixel art (a vector horse is mush at 16 px). **Recommend:** as
   drawn. If you wanted the big sprites as pixel art too, that is a new art style, not "the current" one.
2. **The paddock and the scheme are not in #241's contract,** and both need a little code. The paddock needs herd.js to
   tile `paddock-bay` and place the sign (about 10 lines). The scheme needs a `scheme` key, the 3 serve.css token swaps
   above, and the page setting the custom properties. That doesn't fit phase 2's "assets only". **Recommend:** #244 owns
   the scheme plumbing (it raised the scheme) and #241 gains `paddock`. This issue then ships only the values and
   sprites.
3. **Moments as frames:** #241 says "sprites or CSS animation names". Themes ship no CSS, so I drew frames.
   **Recommend:** `moments.<kind> = {frames: [...], still: "<id>"}`, with the page stepping the frames once at about
   0.5 s each. The red bale's bob stays a generic page animation.
4. **Blocked's red tint:** dropped for the rear, so the coat and fleet colour show; it stays for dead. **Recommend:** as
   drawn. The alternative keeps today's tint on blocked too, which turns every rearing horse the same brick red.
5. **Amber moves to orange** (`#f29b4b`) in the farm scheme, so a working status never looks like a wheat control. A
   status colour shift is visible on every tab. **Recommend:** accept it. Otherwise the wheat accent would need to go
   paler (around `#efdca0`), and controls would lose their pop.

`src/` net +0: this is a design lane (docs only). Phase 2's code sits in #241/#244 as above.
