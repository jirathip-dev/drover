garden theme · design mockup (#423)

1 · the shop, 390 px: sun · overcast · rain

One page per fleet, as today: that repo's corner of the shop, its shelves edged in its #162 colour. Under it, the counter: one cutting in a jar per queued issue. Then the bands: being tended (working, the keeper by role beside the issue's plant at its stage), resting (idle), ready for sale (done) and wilting (blocked). The window's light is the load: sun under 0.5 of the limit, overcast from 0.5, rain when spawns are held.

2 · 1440 px: the shop wall, one bay of shelves per repo

The scene band shows every repo's bay side by side (the existing scene.line), the current page's lit and centred. A swipe walks along the wall.

3 · the window light, the Host miniature and Reduce Motion

Drawn only from what the farm's weather already reads: load/limit and the spawn gate. Sun: blue sky, a sunbeam across the room. Overcast: grey clouds, the room a shade dimmer. Rain: dark cloud, rain running down the glass (two frames, 0.6 s), the room dimmer still. Nothing flashes.

4 · moments
