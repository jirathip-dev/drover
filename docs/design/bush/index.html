3 · the Fire Danger Rating sign, the Host miniature and Reduce Motion

Drawn only from what the farm's weather already reads: load/limit and the spawn gate. Moderate (under 0.5): the needle in the green, a still afternoon. High (from 0.5): the needle in the yellow, heat shimmer over the track, a few cicadas. Catastrophic (spawns held): the needle hard in the red, a heat haze over the sky, the shimmer and a cloud of cicadas. No flames anywhere. The needle wobbles 6° on two frames (0.6 s); Reduce Motion: a static sign. The sign is a board on two posts, about a kangaroo's height; its level word is in the footer line.
