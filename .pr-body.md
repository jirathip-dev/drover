## For humans

Conductors can now run a fixed set of owner-level herdsman commands (cleanup, host on/off, reclaim, account reset, design spawns) that you switch on per group in fleet.toml. Pause and set stay off unless you turn them on.
For a one-off owner-level action (promoting staging to main, `terraform apply`, a host fix) the conductor posts a run card in its thread. You approve or deny it from the CLI or Telegram. herdsman then runs that exact command once, within 15 minutes, and posts the exit code and the output (secrets masked) back into the thread.
Hard nos (printing secrets, deleting production data, force-pushes or history rewrites, turning off protection or gates) are refused when posted, and when a conductor runs them in its own shell.

## What changed

- `[conductor] allow = [...]` (`config.rs`): the groups are `design`, `clean`, `hosts`, `reclaim`, `accounts`, `runners`, `pause` and `set`. Unset, all are on but `pause` and `set`. An unknown group name fails config load.
- Hook (`hook.rs`): a group verb is allowed from a conductor while its group is on (`GROUP_OFF` otherwise) and refused from every other lane (`OWNER_ONLY`). `card approve|deny` is owner-only, and `card post` is conductor-only. `card::forbidden` commands are refused from a conductor (`CARD_FORBIDDEN`). A conductor's group verb is logged as `thread-cmd` with `thread` and `group`.
- The conductor brief gets a "Capability groups" section listing each group as on or off. `roles/conductor.md` Permissions now covers groups and run cards.
- `herdsman card post|approve|deny` (`card.rs`):
  - Posting logs `run-card` with the cmd, why, blast, dir, host and sha. A hard no is refused `CARD_FORBIDDEN`.
  - Approve runs the exact text once, with `sh` in the folder under `--timeout`. It is refused as `ALREADY_SETTLED`, `EXPIRED` (more than 15 min after the post) or `CARD_CHANGED` (the `--sha` doesn't match).
  - Approve logs `run-settled` (`by: owner`, id, sha), then `run-done` (code and a 20-line tail, masked with `lanes::redact`). The result goes into the thread as a system line.
  - Deny logs `run-settled` with approved false and runs nothing.
- Telegram: a run card goes to the thread's topic with [approve] (`card:<id>:<sha12>`) and [deny] buttons, and a tap settles it.
- `docs/conductor.md` documents the groups, run cards and hard nos.
